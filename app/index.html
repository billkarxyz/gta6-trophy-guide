The one missable trophy

Positive Mental Attitude — meditate at all 18 spots. One meditation only exists in Chapter 1: after the Down to Earth mission, talk to Lucia in the kitchen in the morning, accept her invitation and follow her to the balcony. It is gone for good once Chapter 1 ends, and it may be required for the trophy. Keep a manual save before leaving Chapter 1. Every other trophy can be earned after the story. Roadmap to the platinum

One playthrough is enough — there is no difficulty setting, no New Game+, and no mission replay, so keep a manual save at the start and end of every chapter (the game has 15 manual slots). Cheats disable trophies and saving, so keep them off your platinum save.

1 Story + side content as you go 60–80 hours

Play the 5 chapters and enjoy the story — progress made during it already counts toward the 100% checklist, which you cannot see until the story ends. Do the Chapter 1 meditation at Jason's house (see the missable warning above), scan vehicles and study animals as you travel, and take optional missions as they appear: you need any 100 of them for 100%, and there are far more than 100. The 12 story trophies all pop here, plus the five character questlines (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone) and Cal's fishing challenge.

Earns: the 12 story trophies, Road to Rehabilitation, This Was No Boat Accident, The Lone Rider, Supply & Demand, Spin On This!, Baited My Tackle, Stick Fiddler, Master of None, Whip It Good, Let's Go Bowling, Bad Luck, I Guess — and the Chapter 1 meditation for Positive Mental Attitude.

2 100% Completion checklist + challenges ~100 hours

After the story you return to free-roam and the checklist opens under Progress > Stats > 100% Completion. It is closer to 60% true completion: most side quests are not required, collectibles are only partly required, and you need any 70 of 80 challenges. Only Robberies, Underwater Discoveries, Shooting Ranges, Drone Races and Bret's Quests demand everything; you need 200 of 277 vehicles scanned, 100 of 255 animals studied and 300 stunts. Nothing on the checklist is missable.

Earns: A Long Road (gold) plus Cunning Stunts, The Shootist, Top Fun, Earn Cash, Not Kudos, Don't Touch My Deck, You WAiNKer!, One Last Thing, Fair Game, Maximum Withdrawal, A Classic Blitz Play, The Goodtime State, Pest Control, Dime on the Dollar, Treat Yo Self, Little Boxes, On the Spot and most of the rest.

3 Strangers & Freaks + cleanup ~10 hours

Stranger strands are not part of the 100% checklist, so finish 8 of them now — Kirk's Skunk Ape hunt, Kyle's farm, Yago's racing and Pilot Pete's deliveries are confirmed to count, while the questlines of Moses, Tyrone, Yasmani, Andy, Billy and Eddie do not. Then pick off whatever is left: the UFO easter egg, the rooftop cocktail, the blue Banshee showroom smash, max Strength on both protagonists, and the bike-kick and profile-action trophies.

Earns: Strangers & Freaks (silver), The Truth Is Out There, In The Beginning…, Capital Offense, Personal Best, Ten Pin, Actions Speak Louder, Gunslinger, Catch Me If You Can, Challenging Tank, Sneakthief, Hot Together, Retail Therapy.

★ Platinum — Vice Got a Price!

With the other 54 earned, the platinum pops on its own. PowerPyx rates the run 4/10 and about 200 hours; Push Square puts it closer to 250. Either way, it is a long, relaxed hunt with one missable trophy and no grinding wall at the end.
